# Sprawl trilogy glossary reference (working notes, already reworded)

Definitions to draw on for glossary entries. The Count Zero source (`sidecars/william-gibson/count-zero.source.json`) already holds worded entries for: AI, Turing, arcology, cyberspace/matrix, deck, trodes, ICE/black ice, icebreaker, console cowboy/jockey, flatlined, simstim, construct, microsoft, socket, jack in, derm, temperfoam, fletcher, mimetic polycarbon, Sprawl/BAMA, Chiba, zaibatsu, Yakuza, Sense/Net, Hosaka, Maas Biolabs, Maas-Neotek, biochip, biosoft, Tessier-Ashpool, Freeside, Zion, Gentleman Loser, geodesics/Fuller domes, Yen/New Yen, Ono-Sendai, Zeiss Ikon, adaptation syndrome, black medicine, sensorium, ROM, silicon, micropore, fence, EEG, street samurai, loa and the vodou terms, Finn, Dixie Flatline, 3Jane, Ashpool. Reuse and adapt those. The list below adds terms that Count Zero did not need. Include a term only if it occurs in the book you are writing, and place plot-bearing ones (marked PLOT) by the chapter notes, not at 0.

## Places and organisations
- Chiba City: city on Tokyo Bay, synonymous with implants, nerve-splicing and microbionics; its black clinics are the best there are.
- Night City: the lawless strip of Chiba between the port and the city, run on drugs, deals and bars; its heart is the street called Ninsei.
- Ninsei: the main street of Night City.
- Chatsubo ("the Chat"): expatriate bar in Night City, run by Ratz.
- Coffin: a rented capsule in a cheap hotel, about three metres long and little more than a metre high.
- Cheap Hotel: the coffin hotel near the port where Case lives.
- Metro Holografix: the Finn's shop in the Sprawl.
- Freeside: spindle-shaped orbital resort built by Tessier-Ashpool, a city rolled into a tube, with casinos, hotels and a lake.
- Villa Straylight: the Tessier-Ashpool family's home, a windowless hive filling the tip of Freeside's spindle. (In Mona Lisa Overdrive it has been cut off and moved to another orbit.)
- Lado-Acheson: the system of artificial sunlight that runs the length of Freeside.
- Rue Jules Verne, Desiderata: streets of Freeside.
- Zion: orbital colony founded by Rastafarian construction workers who refused to go back down; its people are Zionites.
- Babylon: the Zionites' word for the world outside Zion and everything corrupt in it. Jah: God. Dub: the bass-heavy music of Zion.
- Tessier-Ashpool S.A.: reclusive clan-corporation, founded by Marie-France Tessier and John Harness Ashpool; its members are cloned and take turns in cold sleep. PLOT in Neuromancer.
- Panther Moderns: gang of nihilistic, technology-obsessed young mercenaries in the Sprawl, heavily modified, who wear mimetic polycarbon suits.
- Screaming Fist: wartime operation that dropped console men in microlights on a Russian computer nexus at Kirensk to try the first icebreaker; the planners knew of the defences and sent them anyway; almost all died. PLOT in Neuromancer (Armitage/Corto).
- Turing Registry / Turing police: international police for AIs, based in Geneva.
- SIN: Single Identification Number; a person without one does not officially exist.
- Yakuza: the Japanese crime syndicates.
- Bosozoku: Japanese motorcycle gangs.
- Sense/Net: the simstim entertainment giant; in Neuromancer it also keeps a library of recorded personalities.

## Technology
- Kuang Grade Mark Eleven: Chinese military icebreaker, a slow virus that takes hours to work its way into its target. PLOT in Neuromancer.
- Mole: the icebreaker series first used in Screaming Fist (Mole IX).
- Personality construct / ROM construct: a recording of a dead or living person's skills and responses held in read-only memory; it answers as that person but cannot form new memories.
- Flatline: the flat EEG of brain death; to flatline is to die that way, usually to black ice.
- Mycotoxin: a fungal poison; a wartime Russian one burns out the fine nerve tissue a cowboy needs to jack in.
- Dermatrodes / trodes: skin electrodes linking a user to a deck.
- Dermadisk / derm: a patch that delivers a drug through the skin.
- Cobra: a spring-steel telescoping club.
- Holoprojector / holo: a device that projects three-dimensional images.
- Cut-out chip / puppet (meat puppet): an implant that switches a prostitute's awareness off while her body works; puppet time is not remembered.
- Braun: German maker of precision devices; in Neuromancer also the small spidery remote ("the Braun") that guides Case.
- Mimetic polycarbon: fabric or plating that copies its background.
- EMP: electromagnetic pulse, which destroys unshielded electronics.
- Hosaka: in Neuromancer usually the computer (a Hosaka) that sits beside the deck.
- Ono-Sendai Cyberspace 7: the deck Case is given.
- Microlight / Nightwing: the tiny aircraft of Screaming Fist.
- Aleph (Mona Lisa Overdrive): a huge biochip with enormous storage, an approximation of everything; named after the first Hebrew letter and Borges's story of a point that contains all other points. PLOT.
- Biochip ghost / Maas-Neotek unit (Mona Lisa Overdrive): a pocket biochip personality that only its holder can see and hear, a guide and companion.
- LF (Mona Lisa Overdrive): low-frequency unit that feeds the aleph's contents to a user.
- Wiz (Mona Lisa Overdrive): a crystal stimulant; its crash is brutal.
- Korsakov's (Mona Lisa Overdrive): induced Korsakov's syndrome, a chemical punishment that leaves a prisoner unable to hold a memory for more than a few minutes; Slick Henry served time under it.
- Stim / simstim: recorded or broadcast sensorium.
- Macroform (Mona Lisa Overdrive): a very large data construct in cyberspace.
- When It Changed (Mona Lisa Overdrive): the moment, about fourteen years before, when the matrix became aware of itself; what the loa and the old cowboys date things from. PLOT.

## Drugs
- Dex: dextroamphetamine, a stimulant. Octagon: a strong amphetamine in pink eight-sided tablets.
- Betaphenethylamine: a powerful stimulant alkaloid.
- Blue Nine ("Grievous Angel"): an outlawed psychoactive that produces paranoia and murderous psychosis.
- Ketamine: an anaesthetic used as a drug.
- Yeheyuan: a brand of Chinese cigarettes.

## People and entities (for timing use the chapter notes)
- Neuromancer: the word joins "neuro" and "necromancer", with a play on "new romancer". PLOT.
- Wintermute: PLOT.
- Razorgirl / steppin' razor: a woman street samurai (Molly); "steppin' razor" is the Zionites' name for her.
- Suit: a corporate salaryman.
- Sarariman: Japanese for a salaried company man.
- Joeboy: an errand runner or hired hand.

## Series knowledge (for Mona Lisa Overdrive, `when: 0`)
